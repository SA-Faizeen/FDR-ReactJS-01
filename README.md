# Croix Du Nord

Croix du Nord (Japanese: クロワデュノール, Hepburn: Kurowa Dyu Nōru; foaled March 21, 2022) is an active Japanese [Thoroughbred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoroughbred) racehorse best known for winning the 2024 [Hopeful Stakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopeful_Stakes_(Japan)) and the 2025 [Tōkyō Yūshun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dky%C5%8D_Y%C5%ABshun). He won the JRA Award for Best Two-Year-Old Colt in 2024.

His name means "northern cross" in French.
